## Human review

- Heading: A person sees the exceptions
- Body: The model proposes. It does not get the last word on a low-confidence field or on an action you mark as sensitive: sending a message, moving money, changing a customer record.

  - The reviewer sees the source document next to the field
  - Nothing reaches the system of record until the rule for that field says it can
